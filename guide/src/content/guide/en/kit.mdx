## Print system

One print system builds brochures, catalogues and datasheets, so they read as one family. A machine brochure has three A4 pages, a datasheet four; a catalogue repeats the inner pages once per system.

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Cover** | Brand blue head with the white logo, the product name and one paragraph; the picture full bleed under it; three facts at the foot, each an icon and one line. |
| **Logo** | AIS Vision Systems 20 mm wide, Rosepetal 38 mm, on A4. It signs the piece; it never leads it. |
| **Inspection page** | A headline and an intro, then a picture tile for each detection with its name under it, cropped on the defect and grouped under small headings. |
| **Equipment page** | One large photograph, the specifications in a [specification block](/en/communications/#specification-block), the options as hexagon icons, then the contact band. |
| **Pages** | Calm: white space instead of rules, hairlines only where a list needs them, one accent colour, nothing under 8.5 pt, no large pale panels with a small picture floating in them. |
| **Cover picture** | The sharpest, best-composed image the piece has: 240 ppi or more at print size, never a video frame, never soft or motion-blurred. A machine on a cover is working, with its product on the line. |
| **Rebuilds** | A rebuilt piece follows its original: the same pages, products and picture subjects and count. Restore the original’s figures with their conditions; drop superlatives. |

Every picture is a real photograph, one of our product images or exterior machine views, or a generated or composited image that passes the checks in [Imagery](/en/imagery/#generated-and-composited-images). Client logos, wineries and appellations are blurred.

## Sales documents

A proposal for one line (what we install, what it costs and on what terms) and a customer case study, both on the print system. The proposal’s customer and prices are examples: replace them all before it goes to a buyer. A case study goes out only with the customer’s written approval.

## Presentations

Slides open on brand blue with the company line, say one thing each and close with one next step. Every deck is built from one template; the logo on a 1920 px slide is 140 px wide, the type scale follows [Documents and presentations](/en/communications/), and the hexagon pattern fades from one corner. The PPTX is the editable version.

## Office kit

The letterhead is small and calm, with one hexagon fade in a corner. To write a letter in Word, start from the letter template; it carries the guide’s styles and logo, so type into the placeholders. To edit a deck in PowerPoint, start from the PPTX of the closest presentation above.

<KitFinal lang="en" family="office" />

Before opening the Office templates, install the three fonts in `/kit/office/fonts/` ([Inter](/kit/office/fonts/Inter.ttf), [Inter Medium](/kit/office/fonts/InterMedium.ttf) and [Inter SemiBold](/kit/office/fonts/InterSemiBold.ttf), [OFL](/kit/office/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt) licence); without them, Office falls back to Arial. Logos and pictograms are on [Logos](/en/logos/) and [Icons](/en/icons/). Business cards carry personal phone numbers, so they are not published here: ask marketing for yours.
