# Product components

The components and brand blocks of `@rosepetal/design`, the package every Rosepetal product installs, live and with their code. Every product uses them as they are.

## Components

- **Alert** (`alert`): A status message inside a section: icon and text take the tone, the surface stays the card. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/alert/)
- **Alert dialog** (`alert-dialog`): A question that interrupts: it has to be answered to go on. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/alert-dialog/)
- **Avatar** (`avatar`): The person signed in, as a picture or initials. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/avatar/)
- **Badge** (`badge`): Counts and short neutral labels. Status is an icon and a word, not a badge. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/badge/)
- **Breadcrumb** (`breadcrumb`): Where a page sits. Every crumb navigates to its own page. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/breadcrumb/)
- **Button** (`button`): Primary, secondary, outline, ghost, destructive and link, in every size. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/button/)
- **Card** (`card`): Only for clickable entities, summaries and floating panels. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/card/)
- **Carousel** (`carousel`): A row of items that moves one at a time. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/carousel/)
- **Checkbox** (`checkbox`): One option on or off, or several options from a list. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/checkbox/)
- **Collapsible** (`collapsible`): A section that opens and closes. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/collapsible/)
- **Combobox** (`combobox`): Choosing from a long list, with search. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/combobox/)
- **Dialog** (`dialog`): Header band, body and full-width footer buttons: Cancel first, the action second. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/dialog/)
- **Dropdown menu** (`dropdown-menu`): Row actions behind the more button, with named items. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/dropdown-menu/)
- **Empty** (`empty`): An empty container, with a clear action. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/empty/)
- **Field** (`field`): Label above the control, description and error under it. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/field/)
- **Input** (`input`): Text, numbers and search, with units as input group addons. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/input/)
- **Input group** (`input-group`): A field with icons, text or buttons inside. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/input-group/)
- **Item** (`item`): Clickable entities: datasets, models, images. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/item/)
- **Kbd** (`kbd`): Keyboard shortcuts. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/kbd/)
- **Label** (`label`): The name of a field. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/label/)
- **Navigation menu** (`navigation-menu`): A menu of links, with dropdown groups. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/navigation-menu/)
- **Pagination** (`pagination`): Numbered pages under a table. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/pagination/)
- **Popover** (`popover`): A panel anchored to its control. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/popover/)
- **Progress** (`progress`): How far a task has gone. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/progress/)
- **Radio group** (`radio-group`): A choice between a few modes, as choice cards. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/radio-group/)
- **Scroll area** (`scroll-area`): A region with its own scroll. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/scroll-area/)
- **Select** (`select`): Choosing from a short list. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/select/)
- **Separator** (`separator`): Between rows and in toolbars only. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/separator/)
- **Sheet** (`sheet`): A side panel over the page. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/sheet/)
- **Sidebar** (`sidebar`): The side navigation of a page. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/sidebar/)
- **Skeleton** (`skeleton`): Content loading, in the shape of the content. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/skeleton/)
- **Slider** (`slider`): A value in a range. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/slider/)
- **Toast** (`sonner`): Short feedback after an action. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/sonner/)
- **Spinner** (`spinner`): Something working: in its button, or filling an empty area. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/spinner/)
- **Switch** (`switch`): A setting on or off, in a switch row. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/switch/)
- **Table** (`table`): Rows of facts with sortable headers. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/table/)
- **Tabs** (`tabs`): Views of the same thing. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/tabs/)
- **Textarea** (`textarea`): Longer text. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/textarea/)
- **Toggle** (`toggle`): One button that stays on or off. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/toggle/)
- **Toggle group** (`toggle-group`): One tool or option out of a few. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/toggle-group/)
- **Tooltip** (`tooltip`): The name of an icon-only button. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/tooltip/)

## Blocks

Brand pieces built from the components, with no app logic: the top bar, the login page, the confirm dialog and the log terminal. Each app gives their texts and data.

- **Top bar** (`top-bar`): The brand bar: colour, height and logo. Each app gives its links and actions. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/top-bar/)
- **Login page** (`login-page`): The sign-in page. Each app gives its texts and checks the password. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/login-page/)
- **Confirm dialog** (`confirm-dialog`): A question before an action: it runs the action, shows its progress and errors, and offers Retry. Each app gives its texts and the action. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/confirm-dialog/)
- **Log terminal** (`log-terminal`): A console for logs. Each app gives its lines. [Live](https://aisgroup-design.web.app/en/components/log-terminal/)
