The head and forearms use the MakeHuman HM08 body. The fitted garment, its skinning and its neutral fabric texture derive from the CC0 male_worksuit01 in the system asset pack. The cap, mask, shoes and gloves are illustrative. [HM08 mesh](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj) · [System asset pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html) · [CC0 licence](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md).

Create one suit per operator with `createSuit(scene)`. In each frame call `trunk(hip, shoulderCentre, yaw)` before the four `limb(side, leg, start, middle, end)` calls. Positions are metres in scene space. Side is the anatomical side in the bind pose, −1 on local −x and +1 on local +x. End each sleeve at the wrist. Place the glove at the grip and rotate it with the task. Rotate and position the head from the same hip and spine orientation. Keep the workwear texture beside the source. The project can include `suitReady.get(scene)` in its first-frame readiness promise.
