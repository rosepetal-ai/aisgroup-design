# Physical scene review

Use the AIS design MCP for the overlay specification and the studio MCP to search the asset library. Inspect the supplied installation before placing a manufacturer mark on hardware. Commercial scope identifies the equipment in a quote. A factory nameplate needs its own evidence. Use the supplied screen artwork for the HMI.

For a read result, use the SVG produced by the design guide's [overlay renderer](https://design.aisgroup.ai/downloads/overlays/overlay.mjs) with a pass or reject verdict. Render the 28 px chip in screen space at native size. Drive its appearance from the inspected item's read event. Verify a pass, a reject and a seek to the moment before the read on desktop and phone.

## Contact and position

1. List the physical contacts implied by the shot. Trace the reader to its mount, the mount to its support, and the cable from its gland to the next supported run. Inspect the actual mesh ends. Measure floor contact under the feet and alignment between the product, sensor, pusher and receiver.
2. Expose moving bodies and their supports from the scene's actual dimensions and transforms via the development adapter. Run `scripts/safe.sh node scripts/physics.mjs <dev-url> [fromWall:toWall]`. It measures penetration, floating and sudden displacement. Check that the adapter declares every object relevant to the shot. Add a named fixture for contact the box/cylinder approximation misses, such as a cable end or hand grip.
3. Measure both wrists against the product at pickup, insertion and release. Check limb lengths, moving elbow clearance, planted feet and case support over the cycle, including the other filling line. Look at the filmed shot: a straight arm reaching over the whole table can pass a distance test and still read badly.
4. For a full operator, inspect a complete textured and rigged character in the working camera first. The [ready-to-run transfer](../examples/packing-transfer/README.md) includes one with finger joints. Choose clothing for the task. A scrub tunic, a maintenance overall and a gown describe different work. For a two-handed lift, establish the supporting surface, approach with clearance, close both hands, move the rigid load with the grasp, set it down, then release. Sample several distinct weighted skin regions on each side within the physical face bounds. Check that the pusher has the stroke and backing to reach the product before it moves. Inspect the transition at normal playback speed as well as while scrubbing. The [fitted workwear source](../examples/operator/SOURCE.md) is another option when the brief calls for that garment.
5. Before a restyle, inventory each source-specified logo, its supporting surface, and which pieces share the quote’s highlight colour. A supplied animation or brief is evidence of intended presentation. Use photos or drawings for claims about the installed hardware. If a plaque looks attached to a sensor, follow its mount in the close shot. Size the mark to a real support and preserve the other specified placements. Verify that the production player instantiates the marks and that the highlights read in the wide shot.

For a review correction, preserve the failing frame or named measurement, fix the source, and test the corrected piece plus an older reproduction that should fail. Keep the new rule in the plugin entry point or the reference it routes to. Report which checks ran and what the checks did not cover.

### After integrating an approved person

Review the normal and offline player. Trace one item from the moving belt through pickup, regrip, insertion, closure and set-down. At a group packer, account for each carton individually. A pair of outside grips across three loose cartons leaves the middle one unsupported. Schedule a second pickup or model a real supporting tray. Reserve time for the previous case to land and for the operator to return before the next pickup starts. Review the farthest reach and the opposite station. Check gaze, elbow position and wrist angle alongside skin contact. Run the [full-line contact check](../examples/packing-transfer/README.md#using-the-operator-on-a-production-line) against the assembled scene.

### Whole-line clearance

Inventory the real moving products, people, actuators and closed solids. Allow a named pair to touch only where the action calls for it. Expose the actual posed skin, static equipment bounds, product instance orientations, live actuator transforms and support heights. Subdivide long motion steps or test swept segments across thin panels. Replay pickup, stroke, release and return through every supported path and product size.

Run `scripts/safe.sh node scripts/line-clearance.mjs "$URL" 60 "$ACTORS" "$SEGMENTS" "$OBSTACLES"` alongside the grip check. The last three values are comma-separated expected names from the shot plan. The development adapter returns `clearance() → {actors, obstacles, hits}` with each hit giving actor, body part, obstacle, depth in metres and a swept-crossing flag, and `transport() → {seen, hits}` for observed segments and product faults. The checker fails on any reported transport fault or swept crossing, more than 2 mm of sampled skin penetration, or missing expected coverage. Add narrower contact tests for fine parts. A sampled surface test does not establish clearance for unlisted pairs.
