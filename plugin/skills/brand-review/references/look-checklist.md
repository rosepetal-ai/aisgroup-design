# Look checklist

The checks catch rule breaks, not wrong or dull work, so passing them proves little. Open every page, slide or frame at full size and at feed size (about 550 px wide), then answer these in writing before handing a piece over:

1. **What does the eye see first, and is it the one message?** At feed size, the headline and the proof can be read.
2. **Does the picture prove the words?** Everything the headline claims is visible. "Leaves the line" needs the thing that removes it.
3. **Does every mark sit on what it names?** The frame is on the defect, the chip says what was expected, and the verdict covers nothing that matters. Check each one at 2× zoom.
4. **Is there exactly one defect?** No second product that could be mistaken for it, and nothing half-hidden or cut so its state is unclear.
5. **Does it make physical sense?** Sizes in proportion, parts supported, nothing passing through anything.
6. **Does it hold up against the guide?** Less text, one real image, the logo signing rather than leading, photos at their true grade.

If any answer is no, fix it and look again. When the same mistake could happen again, propose a check for the engine rather than fixing one piece.

Never publish anything rendered with `--force`; it is for drafts that still have placeholders.
