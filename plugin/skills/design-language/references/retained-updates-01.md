## AIS-627829799f79

<!-- AIS-627829799f79 -->
**Decorative patterns:** the five approved patterns (hexagon landscape Rolling and Dramatic, circuit globe, circuit traces and scan profiles) are decorative grounds and accents for slides, documents, web, video, posts and stands. Light, blue, deep-blue and transparent variants are allowed. Night is excluded from the public pattern set. One pattern per piece, never behind body text or tables. No glow or gradient fills; fading line opacity is allowed. Preserve complete cell-edge geometry. Patterns are not restricted to LinkedIn.

Source: brand decisions.

## AIS-b722081c133d

<!-- AIS-b722081c133d -->
A matched pair from the same viewpoint and light: the good part, and the same part with one real-world defect such as a missing tablet, a scratch or a crooked cap. Photographed, rendered or generated, the image never contains the detection: boxes, masks and the verdict are drawn on top in the layout with the overlay kit, in brand colours only. Real results with figures always come from the system itself.
