## AIS-9d634a45d86c

<!-- AIS-9d634a45d86c -->
Black is a production constraint, with one exception on screen: AIS website headers on white use the black logo, where it sits with the ink of the navigation. The Rosepetal site is light and uses the blue logo. Everywhere else on screen and in colour pieces the logo is blue or white. The black file is pure black (100% K) rather than the text colour `#0F1729`, because a black-only printer or press renders any other dark value as a grey dot pattern. Logos are flat: no gradients, shadows, glows or 3D effects. Which version each theme takes is on [Light and dark themes](/en/themes/#logos-and-favicons).

Source: guide/src/content/guide/en/logos.mdx:25.
