# Defect matrix

Products and defects to consider for a defects carousel, with the machine usually associated with each. This is a planning list, not proof of capability: a defect is shown only when that machine's articles, datasheet or brochure back it for that product and configuration. A render, a generated picture or an OCR-like overlay is never evidence of a machine reading.

Each answer slide shows one physically visible defect against a clean control of the same product, same camera and same light.

## Before a defect goes on a slide

1. A real product reference and one plausible visible defect, with a good control. Food photography on a demo label needs rights cleared, and its credit travels with every derivative.
2. A clean image, a Spanish and an English overlay, and a matched OK/NOK pair. A lineup has one defect findable without its overlay; no box on a clean unit unless it shows a reading.
3. Proportions, closure contact, materials and light checked against real references.
4. Every image looked at at feed size and at 2×: labels, lot and date, barcode, box, caption, language and the machine claim.
5. Source, rights, machine configuration and any open claim recorded privately. Passing a structural check is not readiness.
