Apply the [writing essentials: use «» for prose quotes, UI labels and section names in every language; no decorative separators or English contractions](../communications/references/writing-essentials.md) to this reply and artifact; preserve syntax in code, JSON and raw file excerpts. [Production rules](../_shared/references/production-rules.md): no glow or gradients; mascots and illustrations stay held; no public CAD or mesh downloads.

Rule [AIS-cba1a88e1cbf: Keep it simple: one purpose, the plainest layout, design and implementation that serve it.](../brand-guidelines/references/retained-rules-01.md#ais-cba1a88e1cbf).

1. Read [plan_piece first](../_shared/references/studio-protocol.md), then [brand-video workflow](references/workflow.md) for exact source files and commands.

2. Use the existing template, verified facts and approved assets. Keep unknown facts explicit.
3. Run its text and structural checks, render under the shared lock, then inspect the actual output before delivery.

The render delivers the finished film: the published intro and end card, the footage or recording, and the music. Nothing is left for the edit. Every film of the Rosepetal software, tutorials included, belongs to `platform-film`; this skill keeps the footage templates.

Reuse those approved exports; the older opening recipe is a source for requested changes, not a reason to rebuild an available intro.
