# Print and stationery

Read resources-assets/references/kit.md for the office kit and digital-documents/references/communications.md for printed-material composition. The guide names letterhead and Word templates. For PowerPoint, use the closest deck PPTX export; new decks use the presentation skill. The private business-card sources are plugin/pieces/stationery/business-cards/; do not include them in a public download or plugin upload. Use them as the template and change only the person's details.

## Business cards

Measured from the approved card sources. Cards follow this spec, not the 8.5 pt minimum for printed and PDF documents:

| Part | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Size | 91 × 61 mm, 3 mm bleed on every edge, 9 mm margins |
| Front | Brand blue `#17356E` full bleed with the hexagon field (`hex-card-bleed.svg`) from the top right. The white logo bottom left; on the right, bottom, «Part of AIS Group» (in the card's language) in 8 pt blue-200 `#A7BFE4`. Logo width: 18 mm for AIS Vision Systems, 24 mm for Rosepetal |
| Back | White. Name in Inter Medium 12 pt, ink `#0F1729`; role under it in 8.5 pt grey `#4B5563`. At the foot, 9 mm from the edges: phones, email and web in 8 pt (1.6 line height), the address in 7 pt |
| Type | Inter 400 and 500 only; no tagline, no labels in front of the contacts, no icons |

A card never carries invented details: name, role, phones and email come from the person, the address from the company.
